# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-01

- **Tips from Tendai:** short pointers the first time you open a menu or get an alert, and a
  nudge toward your next goal if you haven't clicked for a while in the first two chapters
  (with "Show me" for places). Each shows once; switch them off or bring them back in
  Settings.
- A feedback link on the title screen and in Settings, once the itch.io page is up.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25

The first full release.

- **The whole story:** six chapters, from mending the gate of your grandmother Ruth's
  neglected reserve to deciding its future, with three endings (Thriving, Profitable,
  Fortress) shaped by your choices and an epilogue that looks back on them.
- **A living reserve:** water, grass, ten species with predators and scavengers, seasons, a
  drought, poaching, tourism, staff, buildings and the village next door.
- **Ruth's journal:** a diary of everything that happens, field notes for each species,
  records, and the big decisions you made.
- **Nine hidden secrets** to find.
- **Accessibility:** colour-blind mode, large text, reduce motion, and full keyboard play.
- **Saves:** autosave every day plus three slots, kept in your browser; saves from earlier
  test versions still load.
- Runs in Chrome, Firefox and Safari; no account, no network needed while playing.
