# Playtest guide

For the 3–5 people trying Reserve Manager for the first time (Phase 15). Send them the
link and the questions below; their answers decide what we build next.

## For you (before sending)

- Pick testers who **haven't seen the game** and don't play many management games, plus
  one or two who do.
- Ask them **not** to read anything first. Confusion in the first ten minutes is exactly
  what we want to find.
- If you can, sit next to one of them (or share a screen) and stay quiet. Write down every
  moment they hesitate, sigh or ask "what do I do now?".

## Message to testers

> Hi! I'm making a small wildlife-reserve game and would love your honest opinion.
> Play it in your browser for 30–45 minutes, no need to finish it:
> **https://draworanges492-create.github.io/reserve-manager/**
> Your progress saves in your browser, so you can stop and come back.
> Afterwards, a few quick questions (short answers are fine):

1. In the first ten minutes, was there a moment you didn't know what to do? What was it?
2. Was anything on screen hard to read or hard to click?
3. Did any number or meter confuse you (money, the three meters, the goals)?
4. What was the most fun or satisfying moment?
5. What was the most boring or frustrating moment?
6. Did the story pull you in? Which character did you notice most?
7. If you stopped playing, where and why?
8. One thing you'd add or change?
9. Which browser and computer did you use (or phone/tablet)?
