## Description

**Your grandmother Ruth spent her life building a wildlife reserve. Now it's yours — and
it's falling apart.**

The fences are down, the waterholes are dry and the animals are drifting away. With
Tendai, Ruth's ranger of thirty-one years, you mend the gate, coax the water back and watch
the first impala return to drink.

Then the real work begins. Keep the grass healthy and the herds in balance. Bring back
giraffes, wildebeest, rhinos. Win over the village next door when elephants raid its crops.
Get through a drought. Catch the poachers, and find out who has been trying to buy the
reserve out from under you.

- Six chapters of story, with choices that shape one of three endings
- Ten species, with predators, scavengers and seasons that matter
- Ruth's old journal: your diary, field notes on every species, records and the decisions
  you've made
- A few secrets for the curious
- Colour-blind mode, large text, reduce motion and full keyboard play
- Saves in your browser; no account, no internet needed while playing

**Controls:** drag or WASD to move, scroll to zoom, click to inspect, Space to pause, 1–3
for speed. Tab and Enter work everywhere.

_Animal behaviour in the game is simplified for play. It is a game, not a field guide._
